# ADR 030 remediation

Places where the code does not meet [ADR 030](../030-batch-ai-only-when-an-automation-opts-in-end-the-run-while-it-waits-and-bill-every-batch.md)
yet (ADR 001 §1.6).

- `packages/realm-server/handlers/handle-ai-batches.ts` breaks §1.4. A batch is recorded only after OpenRouter answers its submit, so a submit whose outcome is unknown (the call times out or its answer can't be read after OpenRouter accepted it, or the process stops between the answer and the insert) can leave a batch that is paid for with no row to bill it from. The submit logs and reports such a call as `batch submit outcome unknown`, but nothing reconciles OpenRouter's batch list against `ai_batches`.
