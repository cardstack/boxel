# Choreo gallery: room and wayfinding

The room is an open-ceiling, six-bay exhibition wrapped around a clear central aisle. It presents UX motion, continuity, choreography, scroll/content, spatial computing, and filmmaking as distinct collections in one space.

Each bay is a tangent wall on the same 5,000-unit-radius arc. The 28-degree spacing determines panel width, so adjoining wall edges meet rather than overlapping with projecting fins. Large suspended canopy planes are removed: they crossed the camera and obscured signs. The floor sits below the lowest expanded tile with 160 units of clearance, rather than cutting through the bottom row.

Section names are mounted signboards, not screen overlays: 2,160 × 216 world units, centered on each bay, 26 units in front of the wall. Every sign shares its bay's rotation. A number and discipline label accompany the section name. The lower edge is above the top tile row, and the warm lighting strip is above the sign.

Keep the asymmetrical masonry tile sizes and existing tile/camera positions. Architecture consists of planes only. Warm plaster, dark metal signs, stone paving, and a central timber inlay establish hierarchy without competing with the demos. The welcome panel appears only in the initial overview and clears when a section is selected.
