# TOWERS — voiceover script

The film is at `/_towers`, cut by `test-app/app/components/tower-film.gts`.
This file is GENERATED from the `vo` field on each beat in that component.
Do not edit it by hand: change the beat and regenerate, or the narration
and the shot list drift apart, which on a piece cut this tightly shows
inside one chapter.

## Voice

British English, Asian-American speaker. One voice throughout.

Dry, unhurried, certain. A copywriter reading their own work aloud, not a
documentary narrator performing wonder. Short declaratives. Full stops
where a comma would be softer. No lift at the end of a line and no smile
in the read — the dry ones land harder delivered straight.

Numbers flat: "fifteen seventy-six", "sixteen fifteen". Japanese terms
said once and cleanly, never explained twice; the screen is already
carrying the kanji, so the voice never has to spell anything.

Do not chase the pictures. The camera is always moving. The voice should
sound like somebody standing still.

## Files

One file per beat, named for its id:

    test-app/public/towers/vo/<id>.mp3

Per beat rather than one long track, because chapter skip re-cuts the
film — the narration has to be able to start wherever a chapter can.

## Fit

Every beat's window below has been sized to its line at about 155 wpm with
roughly a second of air. Start about 0.4s in and finish about 0.6s before
the next beat. If a read comes in long, tell me and I will cut the line
rather than have it rushed — the score can move, but not by much without
re-cutting the camera path.
