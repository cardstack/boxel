# TOWERS — voiceover script

The film is at `/_towers`, cut by `test-app/app/components/tower-film.gts`.
This file is GENERATED from the `vo` field on each beat in that component,
and the headroom column is measured with ffprobe against the recordings in
`test-app/public/towers/vo/`. Do not edit it by hand: change the beat and
regenerate, or the narration and the shot list drift apart.

## Voice

Calvin — Asian, Calm, British, Professional (`p9KVucfSoJI7y6G681mZ`),
`eleven_multilingual_v2`. One voice throughout.

Dry, unhurried, certain. A copywriter reading their own work aloud, not a
documentary narrator performing wonder. Short declaratives. Full stops
where a comma would be softer. No lift at the end of a line and no smile
in the read — the dry ones land harder delivered straight.

Ten beats NAME their term aloud, once, as the first word, and never again
in that beat: the screen is holding the kanji, and a narrator who repeats
a word the audience is currently reading sounds like he does not trust
them. `notes/towers-vo-pronunciation.md` is the authority on how each is
said, and the takes were prompted with its respellings.

Do not chase the pictures. The camera is always moving. The voice should
sound like somebody standing still.

## Files

One file per beat, `test-app/public/towers/vo/<id>.mp3`, because chapter
skip re-cuts the film and the narration has to be able to start wherever a
chapter can. The takes that do not name the term are kept alongside as
`<id>-plain.mp3`.
