# Choreo — Interaction, space, story

A 46-stop guided visit through the Choreo Atelier. Approximately 6 minutes of narration, plus camera travel and optional exploration.

The guide pauses at each demo. Visitors can interact, pause, resume a stop, skip forward, or leave the tour. Lower thirds stay on a separate screen-aligned plane.

# Voice auditions

**Shared passage:** Welcome to Choreo. A good animation does more than look smooth. It helps us understand what changed, where to look, and what we can do next. Follow me through the gallery: from a familiar interface, into a spatial scene, and finally into a film.

## A · The thoughtful host

Warm, grounded, conversational. A medium register with a gentle smile. Speak as a knowledgeable designer welcoming one visitor, with curiosity and quiet confidence. Recommended direction.

## B · The architectural curator

Measured, assured, lightly textured. Precise articulation and generous pauses. An intimate museum guide, never a formal announcer.

## C · The engaging storyteller

Bright, charismatic, natural. Clear emphasis and lively phrasing without selling or overclaiming. Let the filmmaking section build energy, then land the final line simply.

## Performance notes

Pronounce Choreo as “KOR-ee-oh”; Glimmer as the ordinary English word. Aim for 140–150 words per minute. Let the listener look before explaining. No trailer voice, exaggerated sales inflections, or claims that Choreo models or renders the world. The voice should distinguish the Motion foundation from Choreo's orchestration.
